# AtliQ Hardwares – Financial & Market Performance Analysis

This project focuses on analyzing the **sales, financial performance, and profitability of AtliQ Hardwares** using Advanced Excel.

The main goal was to understand how the business is performing across different **customers, markets, months, and financial years**, and to identify useful patterns from the data.

## Reports Included

### 1. Customer Performance Report

This report looks at the **net sales generated by different customers over multiple years**.

It helps identify:

* Customers contributing significantly to sales
* Changes in customer performance over time
* Customers with potential for further growth

### 2. Market Performance vs Target

This report compares **actual sales performance with the target** across different markets.

It helps understand:

* Which markets are performing well
* Where there is a gap between actual sales and targets
* How performance varies across different regions

### 3. Profit & Loss by Fiscal Year

This report gives an overall view of the company's financial performance across different fiscal years.

It includes:

* Net Sales
* Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
* Gross Margin

This makes it easier to understand how sales and profitability have changed over the years.

### 4. Profit & Loss by Market

This report compares financial performance across different markets.

By looking at **Net Sales, COGS and Gross Margin**, it becomes easier to identify markets that are performing well and those that may need more attention.

### 5. Profit & Loss by Month

This report breaks down the financial performance by **month and quarter**.

It helps identify:

* Changes in sales over time
* Monthly and quarterly trends
* Possible seasonal patterns in the business

## Learning from the Analysis

Working on this project helped me understand how Advanced Excel can be used beyond basic calculations.

I worked with **Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, formulas and data analysis** to turn raw sales data into reports that are easier to understand.

The project helped me practice looking at data from different perspectives — **customer, market, time period and profitability** — and then using those views to find meaningful insights.

**Advanced Excel | Pivot Tables | Pivot Charts | Data Analysis**
